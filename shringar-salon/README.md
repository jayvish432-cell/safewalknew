# Shringar Salon & Academy - Concept and Requirements

Build a premium, full-stack responsive website for 'Shringar Beauty Salon & Academy', owned by Mrs. Madhuri (Madhu).

## Aesthetic
- Elegant, modern, and trustworthy.
- Color palette: Rose Gold, Soft Cream, and Charcoal Grey.
- High-quality imagery: Skincare, bridal makeup, professional hair styling.

## Dual-Section Architecture
1. **The Salon**: Focus on service menus (Hair, Skin, Bridal, Spa) with 'Book Appointment' integration.
2. **The Academy**: Focus on professional courses, certification details, and 'Student Enrollment' lead form.

## Core Features
- **Hero Section**: High-impact visual of Mrs. Madhuri’s work with clear CTA ('Book a Transformation' or 'Start Your Career').
- **Service Catalog**: Categorized grid with descriptions and pricing.
- **Testimonial Slider**: Social proof from salon clients and academy graduates.
- **Contact/Location**: Integration with Google Maps and WhatsApp floating button.
- **Backend (Admin)**: Simple dashboard to manage appointments and student inquiries.
- **SEO Goal**: Optimize for 'Best Beauty Salon and Academy in [Location]'. Professional, welcoming copy.

## UI/UX Strategy
- **The "Madhu" Signature**: Dedicated "Meet the Expert" section with professional photo. Builds trust.
- **Before/After Gallery**: Interactive slider showing transformations. Highly converting feature.
- **Academy Syllabus PDF**: "Download Brochure" button for lead capture.

## Tech Considerations
- Building a full-stack feel UI.
- Mocking a booking calendar / Firebase-backed form behavior or using a specific package if available.
- Academy Portal placeholder/room for future student login.
